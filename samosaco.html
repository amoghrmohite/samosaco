Our Production Excellence

From ingredient selection to final packaging, every step is designed to ensure quality, safety, and consistent flavor.

1

Ingredient Selection

We source fresh, high-quality vegetables, premium meats, and authentic spices from trusted suppliers. All ingredients meet strict food safety standards.

2

Hygienic Production

Our HACCP-certified kitchens follow strict hygiene protocols. Automated equipment ensures consistent sizing and filling for every samosa.

3

Quick Freeze Technology

Individual quick freezing (IQF) locks in freshness and flavor at -40°C. This preserves texture and taste while extending shelf life.

4

Quality Control

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks for weight, appearance, and cooking performance before packaging.

Our Story & Values

Founded by a team with over 20 years of experience in food manufacturing, Wholesale Samosas Co. was born from a simple idea: make authentic, high-quality samosas accessible to businesses nationwide.

We started in a small commercial kitchen, supplying local restaurants. Today, we operate from a 25,000 sq. ft. HACCP-certified facility, but our commitment remains the same: consistent quality, reliable service, and building lasting partnerships with our clients.
